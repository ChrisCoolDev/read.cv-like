Simplicity is rarely achieved by hiding features behind mystery icons; it comes from organizing information around the reader's actual intent.

When someone visits a personal site, they usually want one of three things:

- To understand **who you are** and what you've built.
- To read **what you think** through essays or project notes.
- To find a **direct way to reach you** or follow along via RSS.

Every component in this portfolio serves one of those three goals—and nothing else.
